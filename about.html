Our Story

Preserving a 4500-year-old tradition of sacred metal craftsmanship

Divine Arts & Sculptures

We are a family of traditional bronze sculptors carrying forward the legacy of the Chola dynasty. For generations, we have been creating sacred Pancha Loha idols using the ancient lost-wax casting process that dates back over 4500 years.

Our workshop is a place where devotion meets artistry. Every idol we create is made according to the Silpa Shastra guidelines, ensuring that each piece carries the correct proportions, mudras, and spiritual energy. We serve temples, home puja spaces, and collectors who appreciate authentic traditional craftsmanship.

In-house customized services include: bronze sculptors, bronze portraiture bust sculptors, marble statue production artisans, silicone mold makers, casting and foundry artisans — all under one roof!
